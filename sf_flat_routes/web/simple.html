      How this works

      Routes are computed in your browser over the whole San Francisco street graph, with USGS 1 m lidar elevation on the Overture / OpenStreetMap streets. Stairs, unpaved paths, freeways and private roads are left out. The slider runs the trade-off between distance and climbing: every route nothing else beats on both, from the shortest to the flattest. Climbing is total gain, not the difference between the endpoints.

      The loop button turns the trip into a loop from one start: the slider sets the distance and the flattest loop of about that length is shown; tick allow out and back to include the same streets there and back. Place search is offline. Source and data.
