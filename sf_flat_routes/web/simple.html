      About Roll SF

      Roll SF finds wheelchair-friendly routes in San Francisco. It never sends you up a street steeper than your max uphill, measured as the steepest 15 m stretch of each block in the direction you travel; going downhill is never limited. Stairs, unpaved paths, freeways and private roads are left out. Slope is the slope of the street centreline: sidewalks, curb ramps and surfaces are not modelled.

      Everything runs on your phone: the street graph is downloaded once and works offline.

      Street data © OpenStreetMap contributors via Overture Maps, ODbL 1.0; the graph is a derived database under the ODbL. Elevation: USGS 3D Elevation Program (public domain). Built on flattensf by Drew Edwards (MIT). Map library: Leaflet (BSD-2).
